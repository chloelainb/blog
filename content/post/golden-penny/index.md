On a dark gloomy night, three of the golden-hearted people were born: one named Chloe, another named Carolina, and lastly Olive.  
As they were being born, the doctors realised that they weren't just any babies; they were special babies.
As the girls grew up, they knew that they would either be treated too well or be treated horribly and, well… I think you guessed it: 
they were treated horribly, and they really disliked that. Every day they felt normal on the inside, but on the outside, they felt 
excluded, rejected, and even like the odd ones out.
On the last day of school, two of them were surprised by being added to the same class. Also on that same day, they found a golden penny. 
Chloe started singing, “Find a penny, pick it up, and all day long…” then all of a sudden, “Chloe!” shouted Olive. “Umm, yeah, what?” 
“Well, basically I heard that we will be in the same class!” exclaimed Olive. “OMG YAY, I’m so excited!” Chloe said in a very delighted voice. 
Then they both sang, “Find a penny, pick it up, and all day long you’ll have good luck.”
On the first day of school, they held hands up and down the stairs, around the playground, and basically everywhere they were best friends ignited 
by a golden penny how cool.They became the best duo in school. this is what happened when the new school new paper come and yes they seeked the third member. 
Three “special” girls—Chloe, Olive, and Carolina—were born to be champions but grew up rejected. Years later, Chloe and Olive found a lucky 
golden penny and formed a group called the Golden Cage. Seeking a third member to complete their duo, they held auditions. A shy girl and a 
“star” failed, but then Carolina walked in. She wanted friendship and laughter. Chloe and Olive smiled; the third champion had finally arrived. 
The Golden Cage was complete, and their luck was just  the beginning.
The next morning, the "Golden Cage" met by the school gates, their golden penny tucked safely in Chloe’s pocket. They weren't just the "odd ones out" 
anymore; they were a team. When the school announced a giant talent competition with a trophy for the winners, the girls knew this was their moment. 
While the other kids whispered and pointed,the golden cage stood tall. They didn't need anyone else's approval because they finally had each other. 
"Ready to show them what 'special' really means?" Carolina whispered. They locked hands, feeling a strange, warm glow—the luck of the golden penny was finally kicking in.


The stage lights were blinding, and the crowd was whispering as the "Golden Cage" stepped out. Chloe held the penny tight for one second before nodding to the girls. 
Suddenly, they began to sing and move in perfect harmony, their voices blending like they had been practicing for a lifetime. A strange, golden shimmer seemed to 
fill the room as they performed. When they finished, the room fell silent for a heartbeat before erupting into a massive standing ovation. 
They didn't just win the trophy; they finally felt seen. As they walked off stage, they realized being special was their superpower.
